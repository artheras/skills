# Motion tokens

Use motion to clarify hierarchy, not to simulate urgency.

| Use | Typical duration | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Micro feedback | 180 to 280 ms | Button, label, or number emphasis. |
| Card or chart entrance | 300 to 450 ms | Use one clear origin and one easing curve. |
| Section transition | 450 to 700 ms | Reserve for a real change of topic. |
| Data emphasis | 600 to 1200 ms | Animate the relevant series or value only. |

- Default easing: restrained ease-out for entrances and ease-in-out for state changes.
- Keep decorative transitions to two or fewer per short video.
- Never flash price, PnL, risk, or trading calls to manufacture a sense of urgency.
- Hold charts long enough to read their title, scale, source, and highlighted change.
