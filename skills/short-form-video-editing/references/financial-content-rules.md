# Financial content release rules

## Claims and provenance

- Record a source and as-of time for every market price, return, forecast, event, and
  macroeconomic claim.
- Distinguish historical fact, model output, scenario, and opinion in the narration and
  on-screen copy.
- Do not imply that a past return, backtest, or model result guarantees future results.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- No personalised buy, sell, leverage, or allocation instruction.
- No claims of guaranteed profit, risk-free performance, or urgency to trade.
- No API keys, account IDs, balances, order controls, or unredacted portfolio data.
- No release when a chart source, asset rights record, or disclosure is missing.

## Required release record

A finance delivery manifest must include:

1. An as-of timestamp.
2. At least one source record.
3. An approved disclosure in the final language.
4. Human approval before status is set to release or published.

Default disclosure:

仅作信息展示，不构成投资建议。

English alternative:

Illustrative only. Not investment advice.
