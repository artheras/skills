# Music rights and mix reference

## Release states

- `brief_only`: a creative direction, mood label, or unverified track; never release it as cleared music.
- `cleared`: an approved track with documented owner/provider, licence proof, territory, term, and a named human approver.

## Minimum rights record

Store an internal or user-provided reference for source, rights holder, licence proof, territory, term, and any attribution requirement. Do not put secrets or raw payment data into the manifest.

## Mix starting points

| Use | Integrated target | Ducking | Peak |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voice-led explanation | -16 LUFS | 6–10 dB under speech | ≤ -1 dBTP |
| Music-led short social cut | -14 LUFS | n/a / automated | ≤ -1 dBTP |

These are starting points; export and platform processing must be reviewed before release.

## Financial-content rule

Music may set pace, but cannot imply a specific market result. Retain an as-of time, sources, disclosure, and human approval in the related video manifest.
